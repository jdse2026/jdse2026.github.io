## Submission Format
Submissions must be in the form of an extended abstract and must adopt the style of the Springer Publications format for [Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS)](https://link.springer.com/series/558/information-for-authors-and-editors). We accept submissions in PDF format, in English, consisting of 1 to 3 pages containing an abstract, the context and research problem, the proposed approach, and the implemented or considered contribution. 


## Reviewing Process
The scientific program committee will review the submitted extended abstracts and will select a subset of them for oral presentations (in English) and others for poster presentations.
