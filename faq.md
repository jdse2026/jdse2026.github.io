# Frequently Asked Questions

<ol>
    <li><span style="color: #3B3FA0;"><em><strong>Can I participate (submit or attend) if I am outside Institut Polytechnique de Paris or Université Paris-Saclay?</strong></em></span></li>
    No, you should be a student in a master program or a researcher in a research lab associated to one of both institutions.<br><br>
    <li><span style="color: #3B3FA0;"><em><strong>What work can I submit?</strong></em></span></li>
    We welcome new results, including ongoing research and extensions of previously published work.<br><br>
    <li><span style="color: #3B3FA0;"><em><strong>What is the required submission format?</strong></em></span></li>
    Submit an extended abstract of up to 3 pages, including references, using the Springer LNCS template, through OpenReview. For full submission guidelines, see the call for paper [here]({{ '/callPapers/' | relative_url }}).<br><br>
    <li><span style="color: #3B3FA0;"><em><strong>Can I attend without submitting an abstract?</strong></em></span></li>
    Yes. Registration is free but mandatory for everyone, whether or not you submit an abstract.<br><br>
    <li><span style="color: #3B3FA0;"><em><strong>What should I prepare for an oral presentation?</strong></em></span></li>
    Prepare slides for a 15-minute presentation, including questions and answers. You do not need a poster.<br><br>
    <li><span style="color: #3B3FA0;"><em><strong> What should I prepare for a poster presentation?</strong></em></span></li>
    Prepare an A0 portrait poster and 2–3 slides for a 2-minute overview. Submit both in PDF format.<br><br>
    <li><span style="color: #3B3FA0;"><em><strong>When should I upload my poster and slides?</strong></em></span></li>
    Upload the posters for printing by 17 September, and the presentation slides by midnight on the day before the conference, using the separate upload links provided.<br><br>
    <li><span style="color: #3B3FA0;"><em><strong>Will I receive confirmation after uploading my files?</strong></em></span></li>
    Uploads do not generate an automatic confirmation. A separate email confirms poster receipt and printing. If you submitted your poster but have not received that email, contact us.<br><br>
    <li><span style="color: #3B3FA0;"><em><strong>What if I have not submitted my poster for printing?</strong></em></span></li>
    You must print your poster yourself and bring it to the conference.<br><br>
    <li><span style="color: #3B3FA0;"><em><strong>Whom should I contact with questions?</strong></em></span></li>
    If your question is not answered in this FAQ, please contact the organizing committee at jdse@universite-paris-saclay.fr.<br><br>
</ol>
